## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* You can easily create the desired ETL (Extract-Transform-Load) flows with drag & drop and simple configuration.
* It provides encryption filters, parsing filters, and other features that can perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* It provides features to run flows periodically at desired times.

## Special Features

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* You can manage infrastructure resources safely and easily with reduced burden.
* Plans are in place to strengthen integration with various Data Platform and AI Service offerings that will be added in the future.
    * It will be available for use in various areas such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Key Features

* You can create flows that transform data ingested from original data sources through filters and deliver it to desired data sources.
* You can easily configure flow logic through drag & drop and simple settings.
* Supports easy integration with other services within NHN Cloud.
* You can execute flows at desired times through scheduling features.
