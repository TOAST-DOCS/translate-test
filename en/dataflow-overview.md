## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create ETL (extract-transform-load) flows with drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters that enable simple transformation of input messages.
* Provides functionality to periodically execute flows at desired times.

## Key Features

* Easy integration with NHN Cloud services is supported.
* Secure and easy infrastructure resource management is available to reduce user burden.
* We plan to strengthen integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * It will be available for various purposes such as business analytics and AI service development.

## Main Functions

* You can create flows that transform data received from original data sources through filters and deliver it to desired data sources.
* Flow logic can be easily configured through drag & drop and simple settings.
* Easy integration with other NHN Cloud services is supported.
* Through the scheduling function, you can execute flows at desired times.
