## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create ETL (extract-transform-load) flows through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters that perform simple transformation tasks on input messages.
* Provides functionality to periodically execute flows at desired times.

## Key Features

* Easy integration with NHN Cloud services is supported.
* Safe and easy infrastructure resource management that reduces user burden is possible.
* We are planning to enhance integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * It will be possible to utilize it in various ways such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Main Features

* You can create a flow that transforms data received from the original data source through filters and delivers it to the desired data source.
* Flow logic can be easily configured through drag & drop and simple setup.
* Easy integration with other products within NHN Cloud is supported.
* Through the scheduling feature, you can execute flows at desired times.
