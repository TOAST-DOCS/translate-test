## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that enables easy creation of desired ETL (extract-transform-load) flows through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters that can perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* Provides functionality to run flows periodically at desired times.

## Key Features

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Enables safe and easy infrastructure resource management to reduce user burden.
* Plans to strengthen integration with various Data Platform and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * Expected to be utilized in various ways including business analysis and AI service development.

## Main Functions

* Create flows that transform data ingested from source data sources through filters and deliver it to desired data sources.
* Easily configure flow logic through drag & drop and simple settings.
* Supports easy integration with other products within NHN Cloud.
* Execute flows at desired times through scheduling functionality.
