## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create desired ETL (extract-transform-load) flows through drag and drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters to perform simple transformation tasks on input messages.
* Provides the ability to periodically execute flows at desired times.

## Key Features

* Easy integration with NHN Cloud services is supported.
* Secure and simple infrastructure resource management is available, reducing user burden.
* We are planning to enhance integration with various Data Platform and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * It will be available for various purposes such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Main Functions

* You can create flows that transform data ingested from original data sources through filters and deliver them to desired data sources.
* Flow logic can be easily configured through drag and drop and simple setup.
* Easy integration with other products within NHN Cloud is supported.
* Through scheduling functionality, flows can be executed at desired times.
