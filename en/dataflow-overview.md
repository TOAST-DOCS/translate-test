## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that lets you easily create the ETL (extract-transform-load) flows you want with drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters, parsing filters, and more that can perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* Provides the capability to execute flows periodically at desired times.

## Key Benefits

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Enables secure and easy infrastructure resource management that reduces user burden.
* Plans to strengthen integration with various Data Platform and AI Service that will be added in the future.
    * Expected to be usable in various areas such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Key Features

* You can create flows that transform data ingested from source data sources through filters and deliver it to the desired data sources.
* You can easily configure flow logic through drag & drop and simple settings.
* Supports easy integration with other products within NHN Cloud.
* You can execute flows at desired times through the scheduling feature.
