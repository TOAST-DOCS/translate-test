## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create desired ETL (extract-transform-load) flows through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters that can perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* Provides functionality to periodically run flows at desired times.

## Key Features

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Enables safe and easy infrastructure resource management that reduces the burden on users.
* Plans to strengthen integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * It will be possible to utilize for various purposes such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Main Functions

* You can create flows that transform data received from source data sources through filters and deliver it to desired data sources.
* You can easily configure flow logic through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Supports easy integration with other NHN Cloud products.
* Through the scheduling function, you can run flows at desired times.
