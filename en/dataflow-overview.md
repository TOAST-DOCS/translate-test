## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create desired ETL (extract-transform-load) flows through Drag & Drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters that can perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* Provides the ability to periodically execute flows at desired times.

## Key Features

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Enables safe and easy infrastructure resource management that reduces user burden.
* Plans to strengthen integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * Will be applicable in various ways such as business analytics and AI service development.

## Main Functions

* You can create flows that transform data received from source data sources through filters and deliver them to desired data sources.
* You can easily set up flow logic through Drag & Drop and simple configuration.
* Supports easy integration with other products within NHN Cloud.
* Through the scheduling function, you can execute flows at desired times.
