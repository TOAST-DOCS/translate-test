## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create desired ETL (extract-transform-load) flows through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters, parsing filters, and other features that can perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* Provides functionality to periodically run flows at desired times.

## Advantages

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Enables safe and easy infrastructure resource management to reduce user burden.
* Plans to strengthen integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services that will be added in the future.
    * Will be available for various applications such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Key Features

* You can create flows that transform data received from original data sources through filters and deliver them to desired data sources.
* Easily configure flow logic through drag & drop and simple settings.
* Supports easy integration with other products within NHN Cloud.
* Through the scheduling feature, you can run flows at desired times.
