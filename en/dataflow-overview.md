## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create the desired ETL (extract-transform-load) flows through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters that can perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* Provides the functionality to periodically execute flows at desired times.

## Features

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Provides secure and simple infrastructure resource management to reduce user burden.
* Plans to strengthen integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * Will be applicable in various ways such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Key Features

* You can create flows that transform data received from original data sources through filters and deliver it to the desired data source.
* You can easily set flow logic through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Supports easy integration with other services within NHN Cloud.
* Through the scheduling feature, you can execute flows at desired times.
