## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create desired ETL (extract-transform-load) flows with drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters that can perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* Provides a function to periodically execute flows at desired times.

## Benefits

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Enables safe and easy infrastructure resource management to reduce user burden.
* We are planning to enhance integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * It will be possible to utilize it in multiple ways, such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Main Features

* You can create a flow that transforms data ingested from original data sources through filters and delivers it to desired data sources.
* You can easily configure flow logic with drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Supports easy integration with other NHN Cloud products.
* You can execute flows at desired times through the scheduling feature.
