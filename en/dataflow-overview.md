## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create the ETL (extract-transform-load) flows you want with Drag & Drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters that perform simple transformation operations on input messages.
* Provides functionality to periodically run flows at your desired times.

## Key Features

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Enables safe and easy infrastructure resource management that reduces the burden on users.
* We plan to strengthen integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * It will be possible to use it in various ways such as business analysis and AI service development.

## Main Functions

* You can create flows that transform data received from the original data source through filters and deliver it to the desired data source.
* Flow logic can be easily configured through Drag & Drop and simple settings.
* Supports easy integration with other products within NHN Cloud.
* With the scheduling function, you can run flows at your desired times.
