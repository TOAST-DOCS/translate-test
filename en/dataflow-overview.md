## Data & Analytics > DataFlow > Overview

* A service that allows you to easily create desired ETL (extract-transform-load) flows through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Provides encryption filters and parsing filters to perform simple transformations on input messages.
* Offers functionality to execute flows periodically at desired times.

## Key Benefits

* Supports easy integration with NHN Cloud services.
* Enables safe and easy infrastructure resource management to reduce user burden.
* Plans to strengthen integration with various Data Platforms and AI Services to be added in the future.
    * Will be able to be utilized in various ways including business analysis and AI service development.

## Main Features

* You can create a flow that transforms data received from a source data source through filters and delivers it to a desired data source.
* You can easily set up flow logic through drag & drop and simple configuration.
* Supports easy integration with other products within NHN Cloud.
* Through scheduling functionality, you can execute flows at desired times.
